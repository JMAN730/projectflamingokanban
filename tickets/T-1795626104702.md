### Comment 9 — @akimfomin — 2026-11-24T05:52:43Z

For the source-only onboarding work mentioned here, [PortBridge](https://akimfomin.github.io/PortBridge/) can help contributors find checks that other teams already ran.

In our first example, [PB-0001](https://akimfomin.github.io/PortBridge/#/problems/PB-0001) credits the independent synthetic rectangle tests on VIctory #346 and puts their test coverage alongside Leonida Continued’s Vulkan work. Each result keeps its original link, exact revision and platform. A contributor can pick a useful follow-up check for their own backend.

[The contributor guide](https://github.com/akimfomin/PortBridge#for-project-contributors) includes an agent prompt and a way to share one result across several projects. Suggestions for fitting VIctory’s workflow are welcome.
