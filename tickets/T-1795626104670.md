## Work

Run `main` on the reference machine, once as a release build and once as a diagnostics build, from boot through story load into the world. Record, as text only:

- the last stage reached and how the run ends (alive, title crash with `module+rva`, device removed with its code);
- shader programs refused and pipelines refused, as counts, with the ten most common refusal reasons;
- draws and dispatches dropped per frame in the world, by the reason our log gives;
- time per frame in the world and the largest contributors the GPU timing switch reports;
- peak video memory and private bytes.

## Done when

The numbers are posted here as a comment with the commit, build profile, driver version and switches used, and the map's fog entries that waited on them are either ticketed or struck.

## Context

Needs the game and the hardware, so this is the maintainer's or a tester's to run. No captures, dumps or logs with game material: counts and our own strings only.
