## What `+0x5149aec` is

slots. The function reads the free-list head at `[pool+0x20]` and compares its low 32 bits with
`0x1FFFE`, the "empty" marker. On a match it jumps to `+0x5149aec`. Otherwise it pops with
`lock cmpxchg` and bumps a count at `[pool+0x10]`. At the stop, `rax = 0x9e0001fffe` (low 32 bits
`0x1FFFE`) and `rcx = 0xdfcf8b420` (the pool), with `dl = 0xc` passed through. The title's crash context
at that moment had 4,904 streaming requests pending, TileHeap at 5,592 of 6,236 MB, and the game frame
at 975 after 9:25. My reading: at 0.1–0.2 fps, objects released a few frames later pile up until one of
the title's pools (one of 8, picked through a table at `+0x8fdce48`) is empty. So this is a symptom of
the frame rate, not a separate bug. I have not identified which pool it is.

- That is about 11 ms of worker time per request of about 20 KB, from an NVMe SSD. It fits writes into
  title memory that is being paged.

One run each, and the table tells you whether you are one translation PR away or one architecture change away (#1226-style in-place binding is the architecture answer at 6 GB, but it is not this-week work). 6 GB reaching story load is also a cohort record worth stating in the table caption: nobody has run this deep below 8 GB on either runtime.


### Comment 2 — @AgneyaK — 2026-11-24T16:52:12Z

Two follow-ups on the same machine.

**1. #55 plus a fix for the invalid call it exposes.**
- With #55 alone the device was removed with `DXGI_ERROR_INVALID_CALL` 1:41–1:58 in, in 3 of 3 runs.
- Cause: the title clears and binds a `B4G4R4A4` texture that #55 made without `ALLOW_RENDER_TARGET`.

**2. The 8:37 run ended at a second pool, not a new bug.**
- `0x80000003` at `+0x1fa88e4` is the same lock-free pool pattern as `+0x5149aec`.
- At `+0x1fa86e1` the code loads the pool at `[+0x8fe1078]` and compares its free-list head with the empty
  marker `0x1FFFE`. `je` jumps to the `int 3` at `+0x1fa88e4` (and again at `+0x1fa8742`).
- So two different title pools ran dry in the two longest runs (9:27 and 8:37), both at about 0.2 fps.
  That fits pools filling while frames are this slow, rather than two separate bugs.
