## Original body

### Summary

In `emit::lower::image`:
1. Comparison gathers `image_gather4_c_lz` (`0x04f`) and `image_gather4_c_lz_o` (`0x05f`) were unsupported, even though `image_gather4_lz(_o)` and comparison samplers (`image_sample_c(_lz)`) were already lowered.
2. Immediate-offset (`_o`) variants of the already-lowered sample opcodes (`image_sample_lz_o`, `image_sample_l_o`, `image_sample_b_o`, `image_sample_d_o`, `image_sample_c_o`, `image_sample_c_lz_o`) were unsupported, because constant-offset decoding lived only inside the `op == 60` arm and `bias`/`lod` indexing assumed `offset_word == false` (and `a16` address unpacking did not separate whole-dword leading address words from packed 16-bit gradients/coordinates/LOD).

This branch lowers all of them cleanly to DXIL SM 6.6.

### Changes (`shader/image-gather-cmp-and-sample-offsets`, commit `572bcbe`)

- `crates/victory-shader/src/emit/lower.rs`:
  - Lower `image_gather4_c_lz` and `image_gather4_c_lz_o` to `dx.op.textureGatherCmp.f32` (opcode `74`), marking the sampler as a comparison sampler (`0x8000`), supporting programmable 2D offsets (`_o`) via sign-extended 6-bit X/Y extraction, passing `dmask.trailing_zeros()` and the comparison reference, and writing all 4 gathered channels (`matches!(op, 73 | 74)`).
  - Lower `image_sample_lz_o`, `image_sample_l_o`, `image_sample_b_o`, `image_sample_d_o`, `image_sample_c_o` (pixel shaders), and `image_sample_c_lz_o` by lifting constant texel-offset decoding (`-8..=7`) ahead of `match op` (rejecting cube samples/gathers with texel offsets).
  - Split full-dword leading MIMG address words (`offset`, `bias`, `cmp`) from `a16`-packed gradients (`dx`/`dy`) and coordinates/LOD to match `gfx10::fields::image_addr_dwords`, fixing `bias` and `lod` indexing when `_o` is present.
- `crates/victory-shader/src/emit/valu_tests.rs`:
  - Add `image_gather4_c_lz_and_offset_match_d3d12`: synthetic compute tests for `image_gather4_c_lz` (`dmask:0x1` and `dmask:0x2`) and `image_gather4_c_lz_o` (runtime positive `(+2,+1)` and negative `(-1,-1)` offsets) with `dxil.dll` validation + container hash check and live D3D12 WARP readback.
  - Add `image_sample_offset_variants_match_d3d12`: synthetic compute tests with live D3D12 WARP readback for `image_sample_lz_o`, `image_sample_l_o`, `image_sample_c_lz_o`, and `image_sample_d_o`, plus synthetic pixel shader `dxil.dll` validation and live D3D12 graphics PSO creation for `image_sample_b_o` and `image_sample_c_o`.
