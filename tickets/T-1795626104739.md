## Original body

Hardware: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4050 Laptop GPU, driver 621.01, Windows 11
Build: diag build (--features diagnostics), env VICTORY_D3D12_DEBUG=1 and VICTORY_D3D12_DRED=1
Title version: GTA6 Xbox v1.0.2.1

What works: boot, first-boot screens, intro, start screen, menus at 30 fps.
What happens: after loading into the world the host GPU device is removed.

Observed from crashes.log (DRED breadcrumbs): device removed after a submission
of 1 lists; 5 compute dispatches in flight:
- dispatch 2x1x1 [compute 91b10a73e48b2192]  (heap 84: null)
- dispatch 1x1x1 [compute 24317e785c8688e3]
- dispatch 2x1x1 [compute b83629b90cd2b7bc]  (Uav s0+0: uav None, heap 0 shader entries)
- dispatch 1x1x1 [compute 24317e785c8688e3]
- dispatch 1x1x1 [compute c1488f485ad606fb]  (Srv + 2x Uav + Cbv)
Error codes: 0x887A0007 (device removed), earlier non-DRED run also showed 0x887A0004.
Debug layer: "Kernel memory failure. There might be a memory leak."

Possibly related: the README mentions compute programs needing 64-lane waves;
NVIDIA runs 32-lane waves. Unverified.

Happy to test patches or provide more logs (shader cache, DRED output).

On your `image_sample_d_cl` experiment: what stopped those programs at `an image address` turned out to be the operand layout, not untracked derivatives. They sample a cube with eight address registers: two gradients per direction (the face coordinates'), then s, t, the face and the clamp, where the translator expected three per direction. #160 has that since `b540f51`.

The `integration/testers` tree ([what is in it](https://github.com/Marvisak/VIctory/issues/293#issuecomment-7121877273)) has #160, #307 and the wave64 PRs (#223 + #233). The same by-reason list from a run of that tree, next to the one above, would show which rows clear on your card, and whether your three pixel shaders are among the 23.

I have no copy of the title; the corpus numbers are EduAzevedo's. AI-assisted.
