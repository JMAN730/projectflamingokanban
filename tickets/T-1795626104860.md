### Comment 1 — @Sunkiller-qc — 2026-11-24T12:54:20Z

The log helps, thanks. Windows 10 19044 plus `+0xb5a5f8` (read 0x68) is the crash from #348 and #741. The in-box D3D12 on Windows 10 stops at SM 6.5 and refuses the root signatures (`Unsupported bit-flag set ... c01`) just before it. That's tracked in #84, and the fix in review is #175 (`cargo xtask agility` loads the Agility SDK runtime).

One thing to expect on an RX 580: Zilumz1 tested Polaris on #84. Agility gets it to SM 6.6, but there are still no mesh shaders and only 64-lane waves, so it will probably stop again later in the game.
