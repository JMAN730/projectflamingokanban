## What

On `main` (e00a0198), RX 9070 XT / RADV under GE-Proton: the title boots, the menus run at 30 fps, the world loads and runs, and the world's picture is almost black — a dark scene with the horizon line and scattered lights, nothing else. No crash and no device removal; the run ended by the title's own suspend at frame 3,592.

Same machine, same Wine and vkd3d, Microsoft's `d3dcompiler_47.dll` in both: a fork base (578d459 / 7b0f736d) draws a lit world at 2.5 fps median with ~5,000–7,000 draws/frame and 0 indirect dropped. So the difference between a lit world and this one is in this tree, not in the platform.
