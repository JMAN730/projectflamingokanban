## Work

The largest single family on the scoreboard: 676 programs are refused because a lane mask is used as a value and the translator cannot say which lanes it holds. Our strings: "a lane mask holding lanes elsewhere read as data" (274), "a lane mask read as data" (270), "a lane-mask join of an untracked mask" (103), "an opaque lane mask" (29). Give masks a provenance the emitter can follow through data joins, so their bits can be materialised when an instruction reads them.

## Done when

The four reasons above fall on the scoreboard, with hardware tests for a mask counted, compared and joined after lanes leave a loop at different iterations, on a 32-lane and a 64-lane device. No program that validated before stops validating.

## Context

`crates/victory-shader/src/structure.rs` turns lane masks into per-lane booleans; `emit/lower.rs` holds the refusals. A mask counted after a loop its lanes leave at different iterations is each lane's own mask, not the wave's: a wrong count there gives no error, so tests matter more than the scoreboard here.
