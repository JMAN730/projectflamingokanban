</html>## Observation

On `main` at `ffe310e`, [`[ir.rs::remove_trivial_phis](https://github.com/Marvisak/VIctory/blob/ffe310eb2098e52169f4c44db5ffdabf43a72622/crates/victory-shader/src/ir.rs#L592-L637)`](https://github.com/Marvisak/VIctory/blob/ffe310eb2098e52169f4c44db5ffdabf43a72622/crates/victory-shader/src/ir.rs#L592-L637) follows replacement chains without compressing them.

PHIs are simplified in physical block order, whereas SSA numbering follows reverse postorder. Reverse-code-order chains of diamonds can therefore produce long replacement chains, even when all PHIs are trivial.

## Proposal

Flatten replacement paths during lookup, retaining the exact semantic representative and existing scan order.

This is a small, iterative change requiring no additional allocation or dependency in the lookup. Compression changes only non-root nodes to their current root, preserving subsequent substitutions, removal decisions and rewritten operands.

## Offline evidence

I reconstructed the relevant CFG, register liveness and SSA construction steps in Python for a restricted synthetic GFX10 scalar fixture, including SCC and both live SGPRs.

For 4,096 reverse-code-order diamonds, the model produces 8,191 trivial PHIs and 12,288 instruction operand reads.

| Replacement-array operations | Original | Path compression |
|---|---:|---:|
| Reads | 16,805,886 | 53,241 |
| Writes | 8,191 | 16,380 |
| Total | 16,814,077 | 69,621 |

The forward-code-order control has identical operation counts before and after the optimization.

All 16 CPU model tests passed, including exhaustive and randomized equivalence tests, synthetic CFG reconstruction and operation-count verification.

## Limitations

- These are logical operation counts, not measured Rust compiler performance.
- Small cases may incur extra operations.
- The attached patch includes native Rust regression tests, but they have not been compiled or executed.
- No representative real-shader workload profile is available, so the practical impact remains unknown.

## Related work

#369 removes CFG traversal allocations; #104 and #697 address DXIL emitter liveness and dead-code elimination; #126 optimizes BDD traversal; #139 caches SSA address sums.

I found no equivalent change in the 30 retrieved PR diffs and eight targeted fork branches examined. This is not an exhaustive duplicate search.

## Next steps

Independent native testing and profiling of `remove_trivial_phis` on representative shader workloads would be appreciated.

A separate dependency-worklist optimization was also investigated, but I would keep it outside this patch until profiling justifies its additional complexity.

AI-assisted. Synthetic inputs only; no game files. Experimental patch, not a verified native fix.
