## Original body

Only resources a shader reads through a descriptor **table** get their contents brought up to date before a draw.

- `tables()` keeps only `u.root == RootType::Table` (`crates/victory-gpu/src/draw.rs:649`), and only those entries reach `readable()` (`draw.rs:1105`).
- `readable()` is the only draw-time caller of `texture::upload` and `shadow.refresh`. The only other upload path is copies (`list.rs:449`, `list.rs:543`).
- So a resource reached through `RootType::ResourceHeap` keeps what its host copy held when it was created (a texture) or when it was first used (a buffer), and it is never moved to a shader-resource state (see also `host.rs:149` and `shadow.rs:13-16`).

**Possible effect:** if world shaders use bindless access, their textures read black and their buffers read stale data. This would fit the dark world in the README. I have not confirmed this in-game.

**Suggestion:** at submission, upload and transition the title-written resources named by the views in the bound heap. A first step could be a diagnostics counter of heap-indexed views never uploaded, to confirm the impact.
