## Original body

`victory census` reports 74 unresolved imports on 1.0.2.1. 72 of them are exports of the
title's own DLLs (libHttpClient.GDK 38, Bink2Scarlett 23, XboxBvhBuilder 7,
amd_fidelityfx_loader_xbox 4) that the loader binds as `title` imports, so they are not
gaps. The remaining two are the only real ones:

    OLEAUT32.dll!#4   = SysAllocStringLen
    OLEAUT32.dll!#6   = SysFreeString

Both are imported by `vccorlib140.dll`, the C++/CX runtime. Nothing on the boot/menu path
reaches them: no image in the package imports `vccorlib140.dll` (static or delay import),
and none of three WARP runs (~6 min, menus rendering) logs it being mapped or loaded.

Ordinal 4/6 are the standard BSTR pair (Wine's `oleaut32.spec` and the export table of
`C:\Windows\System32\oleaut32.dll` agree), and `GetProcAddress` by ordinal 4 and 6 returns
addresses on the host, so a `HostPolicy` entry for `oleaut32.dll` would bind them without a
shim. Worth doing when something starts loading vccorlib140.dll: an unresolved import is a
trap stub by default, so the first `SysFreeString` would abort the process with a crash-log
entry naming it.
