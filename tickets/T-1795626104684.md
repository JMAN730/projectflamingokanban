## Work

About a minute into the world, on every vendor, the title's own watchdog ends the process: the main thread waits on a worker that never passes a gate. Find what that worker is waiting for.

## Done when

The missing signal is named (which runtime call or device method the worker depends on, and what we answer), and the fix is made or split into a ticket.

## Context

Ruled out elsewhere: readback and fence wake-up. Suspects: device methods we answer as not implemented, and the cross-queue wait. The opt-in `VICTORY_NO_WATCHDOG` hook hides the symptom. Needs the game and the hardware; report `module+rva` and our own strings only.
