## Original body

### Summary

In `emit::lower::compare`, the `"u32" | "i32" | "u16" | "i16"` arm handles constant-false (`"f"`) and constant-true (`"t"`) compares via `self.lane(inst, Some(op == "t"))`, while the `"u64" | "i64"` arm fell through to `Err("a 64-bit compare")`.

This branch adds `"f" | "t"` handling to the `"u64" | "i64"` compare arm (covering `v_cmp_{f,t}_{i64,u64}` and `v_cmpx_{f,t}_{i64,u64}`), short-circuiting before `self.src64` so constant 64-bit compares do not spuriously set `flags::INT64` (`0x8000` in `SFI0`).

### Changes (`shader/cmp64-f-and-t`, commit `41715fc`)

- `crates/victory-shader/src/emit/lower.rs`: handle `"f" | "t"` in the `"u64" | "i64"` arm of `compare()` before `src64`.
- `crates/victory-shader/src/emit/valu_tests.rs`: add synthetic compute test `v_cmp_f_and_t_64bit_match_rdna2` testing `v_cmp_f_i64`, `v_cmp_t_i64`, `v_cmp_f_u64`, and `v_cmp_t_u64` against `dxil.dll` validation, `SFI0` `Int64` flag absence, and D3D12 WARP execution.
