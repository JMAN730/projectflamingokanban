## Question

Several hundred programs load a descriptor and use its words as data rather than as a view. The translator cannot always tell what kind of view the slot holds. Should it (a) infer the kind from the table the slot sits in and from how the scalar is used, or (b) change the root signature so the shader receives the console's real descriptor words?

## Context

Option (a) is local to the translator; option (b) touches every pipeline and the descriptor mirror. The scoreboard says how many programs each would recover. `crates/victory-shader/src/resource.rs`.
