### Comment 1 — @pickles976 — 2026-11-24T15:54:32Z

Good news: this is not an allocation bug to hunt — it is a **deliberate suicide**, mapped and cross-verified on the shared kill-map (this repo's #630), and your own crash context names the class it is killing for.

- `+0x23cf683` is the title's watchdog: `mov qword ptr [0], imm32` — an 11-byte instruction *designed* to fault (write `0x4d8bd421` to address 0), not a null deref. Your stack `+0x2215c35 → +0x2215eca → KERNEL32` is the **second mapped site** — the null-callback path via the global at `+0x71b6348` — i.e. the watchdog firing on a worker-thread callback that never completed. Byte-verified across hash-matched copies by three parties including your own fiestorra.
- **Your `Game Crash Context` is the actual finding:** `state Prestream`, **Game Physical 95%**, Pool 94.7%, **TileHeap 3521/6236 MB** — on an 8 GB card the tile heap alone asks for 3.5 GB. That is a memory-pressured Prestream the title kills for after ~1 min, same "dies when streaming starts" family as xcl#1337's budget report: try `title_memory_mb`-style downscaling on your side (the mip-drop knob from your #1086 row 5, and row 8's smaller windows — less physical pressure reaching Prestream).
- **The decisive experiment is one env var** (#917's patch knob): `VICTORY_PATCH=<module>:0x23cf683:9090909090909090909090` (11 NOPs) converts the suicide into a *continuation* — then observe: if frames keep presenting afterwards, the watchdog fired on a **transient** stall your runtime can tolerate; if the process goes silent within seconds, the GPU was genuinely lost and the watchdog was an honest reporter. Either answer changes what to chase next, and the cohort on this exact wall (our #675 crew, same card even — 3070 Ti) has been asking for exactly that discrimination.
- One-line screening before deeper analysis (yours, #1054): a tester with `TdrLevel=0` hits the same stall as a frozen desktop, not a crash log — keep it in the intake form.

Your 8-of-8 determinism with identical pool counters is itself the best datum: this instance is not timing-sensitive — it is a budget that does not fit.
