## Original body

## Problem

While a title runs, the busy cursor shows over its window almost all the time, and clicking the window often turns it into a "Not responding" ghost.

Cause: the title creates its window on one of its own threads, and that thread only reads window messages when it reaches its PeekMessage loop. During loads and slow frames that can be seconds apart. Windows treats a window that hasn't read its messages for 5 s as hung: it shows the busy cursor, and a click replaces it with a ghost window. Measured round trips of a `WM_NULL` sent to the window: 100-690 ms typically, with multi-second stalls during loads.

## Fix

In `crates/victory-hle/src/modules/lifecycle.rs`:

- A title's top-level window is made message-only (`HWND_MESSAGE`), so it is never shown.
- A stand-in window takes the title's window text, style, icon and fitted size. It runs on a dedicated `victory-window` thread whose only job is pumping its messages.
- The title's `ShowWindow` shows the stand-in, and `WM_SETTEXT` is mirrored onto it.
- Closing the stand-in suspends and ends the title as before. The keyboard window menu is still refused.
- The stand-in goes away when the title's window does.
- Frames are presented to the process's visible window, which is now the stand-in.
- Input is unaffected, because titles read it through GameInput, not window messages.

Two files: `lifecycle.rs` (+322/-7) and `crates/victory-hle/Cargo.toml` (adds the `Win32_Graphics_Gdi` feature).
