### Environment
- **OS:** Windows 11 24H2 (26100.4061)
- **GPU:** NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060 (driver 32.0.16.2124)
- **Build:** main branch (2026-11-24), diagnostics profile (`VICTORY_D3D12_DEBUG=1`, `VICTORY_D3D12_DRED=1`)

### What happens
The game successfully passes the initial setup menus and starts the prologue intro ("VICE CITY, TWO YEARS AGO") with working audio and subtitles (~1 fps, 1200–1600 ms/frame, 3D scene missing due to untranslated GFX10 lane mask shaders). Shortly into the intro, the host GPU crashes with `0x887A0006` (`device removed`).

- **Failing command:** `list 26 of 27, of its 21: dispatch 32x1x1 [compute 4c0ac499ad613809]` (`Uav s1+8: uav 4096x1 f41 flags 0x4`)
