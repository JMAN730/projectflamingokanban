- **OS:** Windows 11 (Build [es. 24H2 / 26100])
- **CPU:** Intel Core 13th Gen ES q1hy (`Genuine Intel(R) 0000`, Family 6 Model 186 Stepping 2, AVX2 supported)
- **GPU:** AMD Radeon RX 6800 XT (Adrenalin Driver [es. 26.10.1])

### Test Results
- **Prologue:** Audio and 2D subtitles/HUD text render properly, while the 3D scene remains black. Crashes right after the prologue finishes.
