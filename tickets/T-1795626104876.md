| 7×5×9 3D, automatic | 4 |
| 7×5 2D, explicit one-mip control | 1 |

A native copy into the final 1×1 mip of the automatic 2D texture and a copy back out returned the expected RGBA8 texel on both adapters. The probe covers resource creation and last-mip storage. It uses native D3D12 for the texel copies.
