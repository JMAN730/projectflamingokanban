## Work

Rectangle-list draws that are indexed, or indirect and indexed, are dropped (`indexed rectangles not drawn` in `crates/victory-gpu/src/draw.rs`). The console's tiled lighting uses rectangle lists placed by vertex number, so the pass that should light the scene draws nothing.

## Done when

Synthetic indexed and indirect-indexed rectangle draws produce the same pixels as their triangle-list equivalents on a hardware device and on WARP, and the dropped-draw message no longer appears in a world frame.

## Context

Non-indexed rectangle lists are expanded on the GPU already (`rectangles.rs`, `indirect.rs`). A geometry stage is one known way to place by vertex number.
